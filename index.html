About

I am the founder of Praxin AI, the world's centralized data marketplace.

I started in physics. As a teenager I worked on the LUX dark matter experiment and wrote my first paper in theoretical physics (arXiv:1508.07546). After some graduate research in physics, I switched to quantitative finance on the advice of Prof. Dilip Madan and began deep learning research at Columbia with Prof. Ali Hirsa.

Since then I have run an energy market-making desk and built the deep learning platform at an HFT firm, was at Amazon, and led applied AI for SMB Card Tech at Capital One.
